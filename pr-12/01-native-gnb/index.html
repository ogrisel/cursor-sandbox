Iteration 1: native GaussianNB bfloat16 statistics

The first implementation retained bfloat16 means and variances. On a synthetic 120,000 × 128 classification problem with three-fold cross-validation:

NumPy/ml_dtypes showed that direct low-precision variance accumulation is not portable or numerically acceptable. The final implementation promotes GaussianNB computation to float32, matching the policy used for the linear estimators.
